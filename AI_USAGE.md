# AI-Assisted Research Policy

AI systems may be used for exploration, code generation, literature triage, proof drafting, adversarial review, formalization assistance, and documentation. Their output is never accepted solely because multiple models agree.

Every contributed claim remains attributable to the human contributor who submits it and must satisfy the same evidence standard regardless of how it was produced.

## Required boundaries

- Disclose material AI assistance when it shaped a proof, computation, or manuscript claim.
- Verify citations and prior-art statements against primary sources.
- Re-derive or independently check model-generated mathematics.
- Test generated code and state the exact finite range covered.
- Preserve failed approaches and discovered gaps when they affect the research record.
- Do not present model confidence, consensus, or eloquence as evidence.

AI-assisted work is welcome. Unverifiable work, invented attribution, hidden assumptions, and overstated conclusions are not.

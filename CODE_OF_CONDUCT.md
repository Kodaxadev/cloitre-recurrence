# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project discussion must remain evidence-focused, specific, and respectful. Criticism of proofs, computations, methods, and AI-assisted work is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, ridicule, threats, discrimination, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not.

Participants should:

- distinguish criticism of a claim from criticism of a person;
- identify exact statements, lines, cases, or artifacts when alleging an error;
- acknowledge uncertainty and corrections;
- avoid overstating results or credentials;
- disclose relevant conflicts and material use of automated tools.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces controlled by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct interferes with technical review or community safety. Serious concerns may be reported privately through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`.

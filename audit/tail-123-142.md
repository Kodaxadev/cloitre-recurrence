# Tail audit: L123 through C142

External review observed that the per-theorem "what changed / how it was
verified" narrative thins out after roughly Theorem 122, so the tail of the chain
is the weakest-*documented* segment even where it is well verified. This note
supplies the missing pattern for every claim from L123 to C142, in the same form
as the earlier entries.

It changes no claim. Every status below is the one already in
[`theorem-status.md`](../theorem-status.md); this note only collects the
verification evidence per claim in one place.

## Where the tail sits in the argument

L123–P129 close out the *fixed-ladder* branch of Theorem 121's dichotomy.
T130–C142 then discover that the branch never needed a search at all: the gap and
the block length both have closed forms, so the whole mechanism is a
deterministic orbit. The tail is therefore a reduction, not a new frontier — and
in particular it excludes nothing that was not already excluded.

## The heuristic, and why it is not in the chain

H7 is the only heuristic introduced in this tail. It predicts that no six-gate
all-unit pure-upper chain exists at *any* index, by an equidistribution model for
the successor residue.

It is classified in section C of the ledger, "Heuristic (explicitly not proved)",
alongside the older H1–H6. **No numbered theorem, lemma or corollary depends on
it.** The three other tail documents — `general-gate-determinism.md`,
`block-chain-closed-map.md`, `slack-coordinates.md` — contain no occurrence of
the word "heuristic" at all, and the one section that does,
`unit-chain-determinism.md` §"Heuristic H7", ends by stating that it is
"recorded as a heuristic, not as evidence of a proof".

Its constants are also explicitly *not* fitted: the note records that the model
overestimates the measured length-1 count by a factor near 3.3 at both
`N = 10⁸` and `N = 5×10⁹`, and that the resulting `10⁻¹⁸` figure is an order of
magnitude rather than a bound. That is what the commit message "calibrate the
heuristic chain-count constants honestly" refers to — a correction *away* from an
overstated figure, not a heuristic entering a proof.

## The absorbing-ray material is not a second thread

External review read the commits mentioning the "absorbing-ray hypothesis", the
ratchet, and the arXiv draft as possibly a separate manuscript interleaved into
the same branch. It is the same argument, and in fact the oldest part of it:

* **T1**, the absorption criterion `b_t = c(t+1), c < t ⟺ q_t = r_t`, is the
  first row of the ledger, in `partial-proofs.md` §1, marked folklore. The
  absorbing ray *is* the stabilization condition of the recurrence.
* **T13, T14, T18** are ledger rows 32, 33 and 43, in `partial-proofs.md` §5 and
  §5b. They long predate the safe-map development.

The recent commits touching them are formalization work, not new mathematics:
Theorems 13, 14 and 18 were moved from paper proofs into Lean. The one piece of
new mathematics there is a correction — the ratchet's paper proof had a genuine
gap, since it assumed rather than proved that an occupancy of level `q_u − 1`
must have come from a down-step, and the Lean induction closes it.

The arXiv draft is a separate *artefact* covering T18 and C20, both long-standing
repository theorems. Whether that artefact belongs on the same branch is a
process question, addressed below, not a sign of mixed mathematics.

## Process observations, for the maintainer to decide

These are not defects, and nothing here is actioned unilaterally.

* **Branch scope.** The branch began as "prove safe-map checkpoint monotonicity"
  and now carries the safe-map chain through C142, the Lean formalization of
  T13/T14/T18, reproducibility infrastructure, and the arXiv draft. The
  mathematics is one thread, but the *artefacts* are three: the research chain,
  the formalization, and the paper. Splitting the paper onto its own branch would
  make external review easier, at the cost of losing the ratchet correction's
  adjacency to the Lean proof that motivated it.
* **Tracking.** There are no open issues; everything is tracked in the branch and
  in this `audit/` directory. That is workable for a single maintainer but leaves
  no external handle for reviewers.
* **Review status.** Every tail row still reads "external review pending". This
  note does not change that, and should not be mistaken for review.

## What this note does not claim

* It does not promote any tail claim. K15 and K17 remain computational within
  their stated ranges, and H7 remains a heuristic.
* It does not assert that the tail has had an independent *mathematical* review.
  The rows above are verification coverage — reimplementation, cross-checking and
  machine checking — not proof review. Every tail entry in the ledger still reads
  "external review pending", and that is accurate.

\section{Open problems}

The principal unresolved question is still Cloitre's conjecture: does every positive start stabilize? The result here separates that question from the spectrum problem. Even if a nonstabilizing orbit exists, Theorem~\ref{thm:nonsurjective} remains valid because it concerns the existence of stabilizing witnesses for fixed increments.

Several natural spectrum questions remain:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is the complement $\Z_{>0}\setminus\Spec$ infinite?
\item What arithmetic structure, if any, governs the omitted increments?
\item Can the quadratic finite-start bound be sharpened substantially?
\item Can nonattainment of a fixed increment be certified by a purely symbolic obstruction rather than finite enumeration?
\end{enumerate}

The larger repository develops structural restrictions on hypothetical nonstabilizing orbits, but those results are intentionally outside the scope of this paper.
